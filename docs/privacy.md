# Privacy

## Who is responsible

Firmware Liberator is run by its maintainer, GitHub user
[adamsthws](https://github.com/adamsthws). For anything about your data,
email **privacy@firmwareliberator.com**.

## What is kept, and why

- **Your email address**, only if you sign up (as the tool exits, or on the
  [notify page](https://firmwareliberator.com/notify)), with the models of
  your devices and the references of readouts you sent. It is used only to
  email you as more devices are supported, and about your own devices. It is
  kept because you agreed, by following the link in the confirmation email,
  until you remove it with the link in any email from the project, or an
  email to it bounces for good. An address never confirmed is deleted after
  a few days ([details](what-the-tool-sends.md#your-email-address)). The
  tool also keeps it on your computer, so as not to ask again, until you
  have it forget it (`start.sh email`).
- **Reports and readouts** hold nothing that names you
  ([what the tool sends](what-the-tool-sends.md), [readouts](readouts.md)).

Nothing is sold or shared.

## Who handles it

Cloudflare runs the project's server, database and email, and may handle
your data outside the UK and EU. It logs each request with your internet
address for seven days.

## Your rights

You can ask to see what is kept about you, to have it corrected or deleted,
or withdraw your agreement at any time: the removal link in every email does
that at once. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to your
data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
